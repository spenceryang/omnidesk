# Audio Plan

## Track Concept

Create a rights-safe 45-second electronic pop instrumental that feels like a product launch film and a creator music video at the same time.

## Lyria Direction

Prompt:

> Create an original 45-second electronic pop instrumental for a futuristic creator-tool launch film. Tempo around 124 BPM. Start with a filtered kick and soft synth pulse, build with crisp percussion and warm analog bass, then open into a bright chorus/drop with airy vocal chops used as texture only, no intelligible lyrics. Add cinematic risers and a short glitchy outro. Mood: kinetic, optimistic, precise, creative. Do not imitate any specific artist, song, label, franchise, or copyrighted style.
