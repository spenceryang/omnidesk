# Shot List: "The Desk Wakes Up"

## Scene 01: Cold Open - Scattered Inputs

**Time:** 0:00-0:05  
**Music:** Filtered kick, soft synth pulse, distant tape start.  
**Visual:** A black glass desk floats in darkness. Around it: a phone dance clip, lyric page, waveform, outfit photo, and color swatches drift as translucent cards.  
**Purpose:** Show the creator's raw materials before the agent system organizes them.

## Scene 02: Flash Planning Pass

**Time:** 0:05-0:11  
**Music:** Percussion enters, tempo locks.  
**Visual:** Thin white lines connect the assets into a production graph. Agent labels appear as light strips: Music Analyst, Director, Continuity, IP Safety, Remix.  
**Purpose:** Make managed agents visibly necessary.

## Scene 03: Creator DNA Extraction

**Time:** 0:11-0:18  
**Music:** Bass arrives, vocal chop motif begins.  
**Visual:** A dancer silhouette made of light repeats a gesture. The gesture turns into camera paths, wardrobe shapes, and color trails. No identifiable face.  
**Purpose:** Show originality from creator-owned movement rather than generic prompting.

## Scene 04: Chorus - Scene Worlds Branch

**Time:** 0:18-0:29  
**Music:** Bright chorus/drop, full drums.  
**Visual:** Three original video worlds unfold from the same motion: neon rooftop, chrome train platform, desert signal tower. Each keeps the same movement rhythm and color motif.  
**Purpose:** Show remixability and continuity across generated scenes.

## Scene 05: IP Safety Rewrite

**Time:** 0:29-0:35  
**Music:** Half-time break, glitch edits.  
**Visual:** Risky franchise/style words are redacted and rewritten into original descriptive terms. A "safety pass" stamp becomes part of the beat.  
**Purpose:** Make rights safety tangible and differentiated.

## Scene 06: Timeline Assembly

**Time:** 0:35-0:42  
**Music:** Final lift, edits cut on beat.  
**Visual:** Generated clips snap into a vertical timeline. Scene cards lock into place: intro, verse, chorus, remix branch, export.  
**Purpose:** Show Omnidesk as a production tool, not just a generator.

## Scene 07: Final Frame

**Time:** 0:42-0:45  
**Music:** Clean stop with shimmer tail.  
**Visual:** The desk folds into the Omnidesk wordmark. Small subtitle: "Original. Remixable. Rights-aware."  
**Purpose:** Close with the product thesis.
